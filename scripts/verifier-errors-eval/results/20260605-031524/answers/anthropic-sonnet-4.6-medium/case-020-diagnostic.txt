## Explanation

The verifier performs liveness analysis (tracking which registers/stack slots are live across call paths) and enforces a limit of **10 001** such analyses. The program builds a call tree of depth 8 (entry → a → b → c → d → e → f → g), with each level calling the next **50 times**. The total number of distinct call paths reaching `helper_g` is 50⁷ ≈ 7.8 × 10¹¹, which vastly exceeds the limit.

The fix is to reduce the call depth so that the total number of liveness-analysis passes stays below 10 001. With a branching factor of 50:

| Depth (levels of CALLS_50) | Max paths |
|---|---|
| 2 | 50² = 2 500 ✔ |
| 3 | 50³ = 125 000 ✖ |

So we need **at most 2** levels of 50-call functions (plus a leaf that just exits).

## Minimal Patch

Keep `entry → helper_a → helper_b`, make `helper_b` a plain leaf, and remove `helper_c` through `helper_g`.

-}

This leaves the call graph as:

```
entry --50x--> helper_a --50x--> helper_b (leaf)
```

Total liveness passes ≤ 50 × 50 = 2 500, well below the 10 001 limit.
