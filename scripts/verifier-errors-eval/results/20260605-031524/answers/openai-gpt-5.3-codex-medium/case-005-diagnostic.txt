The verifier is rejecting this because of a **context mismatch**:

- `bpf_preempt_disable()` enters a non-preemptible region.
- `bpf_copy_from_user()` is a **sleepable helper**.
- Sleepable helpers are forbidden while preemption is disabled.

So this sequence is invalid:

```c
bpf_preempt_disable();
bpf_copy_from_user(...);   // may sleep -> rejected here
bpf_preempt_enable();
```

### Smallest likely fix

Remove the preempt-disable critical section around `bpf_copy_from_user` (or move the helper call outside it).  
In this program, the minimal meaningful change is to remove those two calls.
