## Understanding `bpf_dynptr_slice`

The `bpf_dynptr_slice` helper takes:
- `struct bpf_dynptr *ptr` - the dynamic pointer
- `__u32 offset` - offset into the dynptr data
- `void *buffer` - a buffer to copy data into (if data isn't directly accessible)
- `__u32 len` - length of data to slice

The `buffer` and `len` must be consistent: `buffer` must be at least `len` bytes.
