The verifier failure occurs because the `bpf_copy_from_user` helper is sleepable, meaning it may block during execution. This helper is called within a non-preemptible region created by `bpf_preempt_disable()` and `bpf_preempt_enable()`, which is not allowed. Sleepable helpers cannot be used in non-preemptible contexts because they might cause scheduling issues.

**Proposed Fix:**
